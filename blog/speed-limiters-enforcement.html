Enforced Speed Limiters on Public Transport — What You Need to Know

Zimbabwe's Road Traffic regulations require all public service vehicles to install speed limiting devices. Wactrack Investments is a certified installer of these devices, and our technicians have already begun retrofitting buses and taxis with SAZ-approved governors across Harare and beyond.

What the regulation requires

Under Statutory Instrument 118 of 2023, public service vehicles (PSVs) and other heavy or high-risk vehicle categories must be fitted with a speed limiting device that prevents the vehicle from exceeding 100 km/h. Vehicles must carry a valid Certificate of Compliance confirming the device is installed and functioning correctly.

Why it matters for operators

  • Non-compliant vehicles risk being taken off the road during routine inspections.
  • Speed governors measurably reduce the risk of high-speed accidents on Zimbabwe's highways.
  • Certified installation protects operators from liability and keeps insurance and licensing in good standing.

How Wactrack helps

As an SAZ/VID-approved installer, Wactrack supplies and fits compliant speed governor hardware (including Uniguard UT04S systems) and issues Certificates of Compliance on-site. Our technicians can often complete installation the same day, minimizing downtime for operators running tight schedules.
