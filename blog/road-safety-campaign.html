Wactrack Joins Road Safety Campaign

We are proud to partner with the Traffic Safety Council of Zimbabwe in promoting safer driving practices. As part of our commitment to road safety, Wactrack is offering discounted installations of speed governors for registered PSVs throughout July.

Why we're getting involved

As a certified speed governor installer, Wactrack sees first-hand how compliant technology reduces risk on Zimbabwe's roads. Partnering with the Traffic Safety Council lets us extend that impact beyond our existing clients, reaching operators who may not yet be aware of their compliance obligations.

What the campaign offers

  • Discounted speed governor installation for registered public service vehicles during July.
  • On-site compliance checks alongside installation appointments.
  • Driver awareness materials covering safe speed practices.

Operators wanting to take part, or simply learn more about keeping their fleet compliant, are welcome to reach out to our Harare team directly.
