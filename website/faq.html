FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers on GPS trackers, speed governors, installation and more. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

What is GPS fleet tracking and how does it work?+

GPS tracking uses satellite technology to monitor vehicle locations in real time. Wactrack installs GPS units on vehicles; our online platform lets you view routes, speeds, and stops. This increases security and productivity by providing visibility into your fleet's movements.

Why install a speed governor on my vehicle?+

As of 2024, all PSVs and heavy vehicles must have SAZ/VID-approved speed governors. These devices prevent vehicles from exceeding 100 km/h, reducing accident risk. Wactrack provides installation and certification services to keep your vehicles legal and safe.

What do I need for a vehicle tracker installation?+

Typically, we require proof of vehicle ownership (registration papers). Installation can be done at your location by our technicians. We also handle any required regulatory paperwork (like speed governor certification) to ensure compliance.

Do you offer support after installation?+

Yes. Our standard package includes 24/7 technical support. We also provide user training on the tracking platform and offer maintenance services to keep equipment functioning optimally.

Can I track fuel usage with your system?+

Absolutely. We install electronic fuel sensors that send level data to your dashboard. You can receive alerts for fuel theft or leaks, and run reports on consumption to control costs.

How quickly can you install a device on my vehicle?+

Most installations are completed same-day, subject to scheduling and device availability, so your fleet stays operational and compliant with minimal downtime.

Do you serve individual car owners, or only fleets?+

Both. While much of our work supports public transport, logistics and corporate fleets, we also fit GPS tracking and CCTV solutions for individual vehicle owners seeking theft protection and peace of mind.
