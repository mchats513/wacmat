Our Story

A Harare-based technology partner you can trust

Wacmat Investments (Pvt) Ltd, trading as Wactrack, is a Zimbabwean technology firm specializing in vehicle tracking and security solutions. Based in Graniteside, Harare, we provide comprehensive fleet management services — from GPS-based fleet tracking and real-time fuel monitoring, to the installation of SAZ-approved speed limiting devices.

We are officially listed by the Standards Association of Zimbabwe as an authorized speed governor installer, demonstrating our compliance with national safety standards. Our goal is simple: help clients improve efficiency and safety through dependable telematics, backed by a team that understands local roads and regulations.

Our Mission

To empower Zimbabwean businesses and drivers with intelligent fleet technology that reduces costs and improves safety, in line with national regulations.
